Site note / privacy

Nothing to hide. Nothing uploaded.

Last updated: 27 August 2026. Static QR is designed so your content stays on your device.

What we process

URLs, Wi-Fi credentials, contact details, event details, text, logos and CSV files are processed only by JavaScript running in your browser. They are not sent to us or to a third party. Generated files are created locally.

What we collect

We do not use analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, accounts or local storage. Our hosting provider may retain standard, short-lived web server logs such as IP address, request path and browser user agent for security and reliability. Those requests do not contain QR payloads.

Offline cache

After a visit, your browser may cache the application shell so the generator works offline. The cache contains application files, not your QR content. You can remove it through your browser’s site-data settings.

Your control

Close or refresh the page to clear entered content from memory. Because we do not receive your payloads, we cannot retrieve or delete them for you.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
